New Vanguard Research · September 2026

The AI advice frontier: Use, trust, and the human edge

with Andy Reed, Nathan Young, Samantha Ostrom, Fiona Greig

A large-scale study of how retail investors actually use AI for financial guidance. Investors use AI to learn and explore, but they don't trust it much. Instead of replacing human advisors, AI makes investors value them more.
